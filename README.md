He offers the hero tea, not a duel.

> Come in, hero. Mind the step — the third one bites; I never did fix it. Yes, the sword, the prophecy. I've heard it three times this week, and it's only Thursday. The crown's on the table. The throne's... somewhere under the laundry. Take the crown. The kingdom comes with it: six provinces, two famines, and a goblin union that files grievances in triplicate. I conquered the world at twenty-three and I've been doing the paperwork ever since. Go on, strike me down. Or — better — sit. There's tea, and you're the first guest this castle's had in years.
